BrightAce Academy country flag font

The country flag artwork is derived from Twemoji and redistributed through Mozilla's
Twemoji COLR/CPAL project. BrightAce uses a subset of the Twemoji Mozilla color font,
limited to the regional-indicator characters needed by the BrightAce country picker.

Source project:
https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr

Subset/build note:
The bundled BrightAce font is a technical subset of Twemoji Mozilla containing only
regional-indicator flag glyphs and their ligature rules needed for country flags.
