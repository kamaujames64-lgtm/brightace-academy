Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about BrightAce Academy and how the service works.

Do I need to create an account?

No. BrightAce is designed without student sign-up or login requirements.

Who will I be chatting with?

You will be communicating with the BrightAce Academy tutor directly.

Can I ask about a subject that is not listed?

Yes. Start a chat and explain what you need.

Can I share an assignment?

The final messaging implementation will support the appropriate file-sharing workflow. We will configure this securely when the WhatsApp Business backend is connected.

Is BrightAce available internationally?

Yes. The website is designed for students globally.

How do I arrange tutoring or homework help?

Start a live chat, describe what you need, and discuss the next steps directly with the tutor.
