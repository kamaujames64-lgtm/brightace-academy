Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BrightAce Academy, getting help, assignments, payments, communication, and academic support.

Do I need to create an account?

No. BrightAce is designed so students can request help without creating a student account or password.

How do I start a request?

Open Live Chat, provide your name, WhatsApp number, describe the work you need help with, set your budget, choose your currency, and optionally provide a deadline. Your request is then reviewed by BrightAce Admin.

Who receives my request?

Your request first goes to BrightAce Admin. Admin reviews the task and assigns it to an appropriate tutor. You can then continue discussing the work through BrightAce chat and WhatsApp when available.

Can I choose my own budget?

Yes. You set the initial student budget when starting the request. After reviewing the task, BrightAce Admin may discuss the scope and agree on the final approved amount with you.

Can the final price be different from my initial budget?

Yes. Your initial budget is your starting proposal. The final approved amount can be different after the task, scope, complexity, deadline, and required work are reviewed.

Which currencies can I use?

You can state your budget in USD, KES, or EUR when submitting a request. The payment method available for the final approved amount depends on the supported payment currency and payment processor.

Can I pay in EUR through Paystack?

For a Kenya-based Paystack business, Paystack currently lists KES and USD as supported payment currencies. EUR can therefore be recorded as a requested budget, but it is not currently a direct Paystack Kenya checkout currency. Admin can agree on a supported payment currency before checkout.

Who should I pay?

Pay only through the official BrightAce payment process. Do not send payment directly to a tutor's personal bank account, mobile-money account, PayPal account, or personal WhatsApp number.

When do I pay?

After Admin reviews the request, assigns the work, and agrees the final amount with you, Admin can create a secure payment request. The student then pays the full approved amount through the official BrightAce payment page.

Do I pay the tutor directly?

No. Students should not pay tutors directly. BrightAce handles the student payment workflow so the payment can be recorded against the approved request.

What payment processor does BrightAce use?

BrightAce's current checkout integration uses Paystack. The payment page creates the transaction from the approved request rather than allowing the student to change the amount in the browser.

Is my payment amount verified?

Yes. The backend records the approved amount and currency and verifies the Paystack transaction against those values before marking the request as paid.

Can I request a refund?

If the agreed service is not fully delivered, a partial or full refund may be considered after review. Refunds are handled according to the circumstances of the request; a refund is not an automatic guarantee.

Can I cancel a request?

Contact BrightAce Admin as soon as possible. Whether a request can be cancelled depends on its current stage, including whether work has started and whether payment has already been processed.

Can I send my assignment or other files?

Yes. The BrightAce chat workflow is designed for sharing relevant academic files such as assignments, documents, images, spreadsheets, presentations, and worked materials where supported. Only share files necessary for the task.

Can the tutor send me worked solutions or study materials?

Yes. Where appropriate, the tutor can send explanations, worked examples, notes, study materials, and other academic support through the conversation.

Can I discuss the assignment before paying?

Yes. You can discuss the task, clarify requirements, and agree on the scope and final amount before the secure payment request is issued.

Will my tutor see my original budget?

BrightAce's workflow is designed to protect the tutor from seeing the student's original budget and BrightAce's internal revenue split. The tutor receives the assignment information needed to perform the work and their applicable payout information.

How is tutor payment handled?

For the current BrightAce workflow, an eligible completed and paid assignment records a tutor payout of 60% of the final approved amount, while BrightAce retains 40%. Tutor balances are tracked by Admin and are not mixed with student-facing pricing.

Does BrightAce automatically transfer money to tutors?

Not in the current workflow. Tutor balances are recorded in the Admin ledger. Admin marks an outstanding balance as paid after the tutor has been paid through the agreed external payout method.

How do I know whether my work is assigned?

Admin assigns the request to a tutor after reviewing it. You can continue communicating about the task through the BrightAce conversation once the assignment is made.

Can Admin assign work directly to a tutor?

Yes. Admin can review incoming student requests and assign work to an available tutor. Admin can also manage the payment workflow associated with the assignment.

Who can I contact if something goes wrong?

Use BrightAce Live Chat and explain the issue clearly. Include the relevant request or payment reference if you have one so Admin can locate the record quickly.

Is BrightAce available internationally?

Yes. BrightAce is designed to support students worldwide. Payment currency and available payment methods can vary by the payment processor and the business's enabled currencies.

What subjects do you support?

BrightAce supports a broad range of subjects, including mathematics, statistics, sciences, computer science, data analysis, accounting and finance, business, economics, languages, research methods, Excel, R, SPSS, STATA, MATLAB, programming, exam preparation, homework help, and other academic areas.

Can I request a subject that is not listed?

Yes. Start a chat and describe the subject and task. Admin can review the request and determine whether an appropriate tutor is available.

What should I include in my first message?

Give a clear description of the task, what you need completed or explained, your deadline if any, the expected format, and any instructions from your school, college, university, or instructor. Attaching the assignment instructions can also help.

Can you help with urgent deadlines?

You can tell us your deadline when submitting the request. Urgent work is subject to tutor availability and the complexity of the task, so a deadline should be discussed and confirmed before payment.

Do I need to provide my school or university login?

No. Do not share passwords, authentication codes, or other account credentials. Provide only the academic materials and instructions necessary to help with your request.

Is my information shared with tutors?

Only information reasonably needed to handle and complete your request should be shared with the assigned tutor. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or confidential information in the chat.

Can BrightAce guarantee a grade?

No. BrightAce can provide academic assistance, explanations, study support, and help with assignments, but no legitimate service can guarantee a particular grade or academic outcome.

Can BrightAce complete work for me?

BrightAce can discuss the type of academic assistance that is appropriate for your request. Students remain responsible for following their institution's academic-integrity rules and submitting work honestly.

What happens if the tutor needs clarification?

The tutor can continue the conversation with you through the available BrightAce chat/WhatsApp workflow to clarify requirements before proceeding.

Can I reopen an old request?

If your request is still available in the system, contact Admin through Live Chat and provide the relevant details. Admin can review the previous conversation and determine the next step.

What if I do not receive a response?

Send a message through Live Chat and allow a reasonable response time. If the matter is urgent, clearly state the deadline so Admin can assess availability.

How can I get help right now?

Use the BrightAce Live Chat page, provide the requested task details, and submit your request. That's the quickest way to get started.

Payment safety: Never send money directly to a tutor's personal payment account. Use only the official BrightAce payment process. If you receive a suspicious payment request, contact BrightAce Admin before paying.
