Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BrightAce Academy, getting help, assignments, payments, communication, and academic support.

1. Getting Started

Do I need to create an account?

No. BrightAce Academy does not require students or clients to create an account before requesting academic assistance.

You can simply start a request through our Live Chat or designated request channel and tell us what you need help with.

We aim to keep the process simple, convenient, and accessible.

How do I start a request?

Starting is easy.

Send BrightAce Admin a message through the available communication channel and provide as much information as possible about your request.

For example, you may provide:

  • The subject or course
  • The type of academic assistance required
  • The assignment or task instructions
  • Number of questions or pages
  • Required format
  • Your deadline
  • Any marking rubric or lecturer instructions
  • Reference materials
  • Your preferred level of support
  • Your expected budget, if applicable

You may also attach relevant files.

BrightAce Admin will review your request and discuss the requirements with you before the work is assigned.

Who receives my request?

Your initial request is received and handled by BrightAce Admin.

Admin reviews your requirements, clarifies anything that is unclear, discusses pricing and timelines, and confirms the arrangement with you.

Only after the request has been properly reviewed and agreed upon will it be assigned to the appropriate tutor or academic support specialist.

Can I contact a tutor directly?

Not before your request has been reviewed and assigned.

BrightAce uses an Admin-first communication process to protect both students and tutors and to ensure that every request is properly understood.

Once your request has been confirmed and assigned, BrightAce Admin may facilitate communication with the assigned tutor where necessary.

Can I choose my own tutor?

You may communicate your preferences to BrightAce Admin.

However, tutor assignment is normally handled by BrightAce based on factors such as:

  • Subject expertise
  • Type of assignment
  • Academic level
  • Availability
  • Deadline
  • Required skills
  • Complexity of the work

This helps us match requests with the most appropriate available academic support.

2. Pricing and Quotes

Can I choose my own budget?

You may tell BrightAce Admin your expected budget when submitting a request.

However, your proposed budget does not automatically become the final price.

The final price depends on factors such as:

  • Complexity
  • Amount of work
  • Academic level
  • Deadline
  • Research requirements
  • Formatting requirements
  • Data analysis requirements
  • Number of questions/pages
  • Special instructions
  • Required turnaround time
Can the final price be different from my initial budget?

Yes.

The budget you provide is an indication of what you are prepared to spend. It is not automatically the final agreed price.

BrightAce Admin will discuss the actual price with you before payment is requested.

You should never be charged an amount that you have not agreed to.

How is the price determined?

BrightAce considers the complete requirements of the request before confirming a price.

For example, a short explanation of a mathematical concept may require considerably less work than a large research assignment involving data analysis, references, formatting, and a short deadline.

Admin will communicate the applicable price before proceeding.

Can I request a price estimate before submitting my files?

Yes.

You can describe your task and ask for an estimate.

For a more accurate quote, however, it is helpful to provide the actual assignment instructions or relevant materials.

3. Payments

Which currencies can I use?

BrightAce aims to support students and clients internationally.

Available payment currencies may depend on the payment processor, your location, and the payment method being used.

Before making payment, BrightAce Admin will confirm the applicable payment amount and available payment method.

Payment availability depends on the currencies and payment methods supported by the payment processor at the time of payment.

If a particular currency is not supported, BrightAce Admin can advise you on the available payment option.

Do not send money to an alternative personal account simply because your preferred currency is unavailable.

Who should I pay?

Payments should be made only through the official BrightAce Academy payment process provided by BrightAce Admin.

Never send money directly to a tutor's personal account unless BrightAce officially and clearly instructs you through its approved payment process.

When do I pay?

Payment requirements depend on the type and size of the request.

Before work begins, BrightAce Admin will communicate:

  • The agreed price
  • Required deposit or payment
  • Payment method
  • Expected timeline
  • Any applicable payment conditions

You should understand and agree to these terms before proceeding.

Do I pay the tutor directly?

No.

Clients should not negotiate private payments directly with tutors.

BrightAce's Admin-managed payment process helps maintain proper records and protects both the client and the academic support provider.

If someone asks you to pay them privately outside the approved BrightAce process, contact BrightAce Admin before sending any money.

What payment processor does BrightAce use?

BrightAce may use approved third-party payment processors to process payments.

The payment processor available to you may depend on your country, currency, payment method, and current processor availability.

BrightAce will communicate the approved payment route before payment.

Is my payment amount verified?

Yes.

Before payment, the agreed amount should be confirmed by BrightAce Admin or the official payment process.

If the amount shown during payment does not match the amount you were told to pay, stop and contact BrightAce Admin before completing the transaction.

Does BrightAce automatically transfer money to tutors?

Clients should not assume that payment made to BrightAce is automatically transferred directly to a tutor.

Tutor compensation is handled internally by BrightAce according to its arrangements with the academic support provider.

The client therefore deals with BrightAce rather than having to manage tutor payments independently.

Is my payment refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on the circumstances of the request and the applicable BrightAce payment terms.

If you believe you are entitled to a refund, contact BrightAce Admin as soon as possible and provide:

  • Your request details
  • Payment information or transaction reference
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Any relevant communication or evidence

Each case may be reviewed individually.

4. Cancelling a Request

Can I cancel a request?

You may request cancellation by contacting BrightAce Admin.

However, cancellation terms may depend on whether:

  • Work has already started
  • A tutor has already been assigned
  • Research or preparation has already been completed
  • The deadline is approaching
  • A deposit has already been used for the request

Contact Admin as early as possible if you need to cancel.

What happens if I change my requirements after agreeing to the price?

Tell BrightAce Admin immediately.

A significant change in requirements may affect:

  • Price
  • Deadline
  • Tutor assignment
  • Scope of work

BrightAce will discuss any significant changes with you before proceeding.

6. Online Tutoring Classes

Does BrightAce offer live online tutoring?

Yes.

BrightAce Academy offers live online tutoring classes for students who prefer personalised, real-time instruction and interaction with a tutor.

Classes are conducted online using Zoom, allowing students and tutors to communicate face-to-face, share screens, work through questions, and discuss difficult concepts in real time.

How do BrightAce online tutoring classes work?

The process is simple:

  1. Contact BrightAce Admin through Live Chat or the available communication channel.
  2. Tell us the subject, academic level, topics, and type of tutoring you need.
  3. Propose your preferred days and times for the tutoring sessions.
  4. Discuss and agree on the hourly tutoring rate with BrightAce Admin.
  5. Once the schedule, hourly rate, and other arrangements are agreed upon, your tutoring sessions are organised.
  6. You receive the relevant Zoom class details for the agreed session.
  7. Join the class at the scheduled time and learn directly with your tutor.

This gives students flexibility while allowing BrightAce to coordinate the tutoring arrangement properly.

Can I choose the day and time for my tutoring class?

Yes.

Students or clients can propose their preferred schedule and time.

For example, you may request:

  • Monday at 5:00 PM
  • Wednesday at 7:00 PM
  • Saturday morning
  • Two sessions per week
  • A specific number of hours per week

BrightAce will check tutor availability and work with you to agree on a suitable schedule.

Your proposed time is not automatically confirmed until availability has been checked and the session has been agreed upon.

How much does online tutoring cost?

Online tutoring is generally arranged on an hourly basis.

You can discuss your preferred tutoring requirements with BrightAce Admin, and the hourly rate will be agreed before the tutoring sessions begin.

The total cost will depend on:

  • Agreed hourly rate
  • Number of hours
  • Number of sessions
  • Frequency of classes
  • Subject and academic level
  • Any specialised tutoring requirements
Can I decide how many hours I want?

Yes.

You can propose the amount of tutoring time you believe you need.

For example, you may request:

  • One hour per session
  • Two-hour sessions
  • Several sessions per week
  • Weekly tutoring
  • Short-term intensive tutoring
  • Ongoing tutoring throughout a course

The final schedule depends on your requirements and tutor availability.

Do I have to commit to a long-term tutoring package?

Not necessarily.

Tutoring arrangements can be discussed based on your individual needs.

You may require help with a single topic, a particular examination, a short course, or ongoing academic support.

BrightAce Admin will discuss the available arrangement with you before you commit.

Can I have tutoring for a specific topic rather than an entire subject?

Yes.

What personal information should I avoid sending?
