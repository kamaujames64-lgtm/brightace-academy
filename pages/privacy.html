Privacy Policy

How BrightAce Academy handles information when you use our website, live chat, messaging, and payment services.

Effective date: September 2026

BrightAce Academy ("BrightAce", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we use it, how it may be shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BrightAce, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, WhatsApp/telephone number, and email address when provided.
  • Academic request information: subject, assignment or work description, deadline, student budget, agreed service details, and related messages.
  • Files and attachments: documents, images, spreadsheets, presentations, or other files that you voluntarily send through BrightAce messaging.
  • Payment information: payment request details, transaction references, amount, currency, and payment status. Payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment processor rather than stored by BrightAce in the ordinary website workflow.
  • Technical information: information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and connected services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Receive and respond to academic help requests.
  • Communicate with students about requested services.
  • Coordinate assigned academic work with authorized tutors.
  • Process and verify payments.
  • Review refund requests and service-related disputes.
  • Deliver requested academic materials and files.
  • Maintain records for service administration, security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of BrightAce services.

3. Live Chat and WhatsApp

BrightAce may use WhatsApp Business Platform services to facilitate communication between students and BrightAce. Information you send through live chat or connected messaging may be processed by BrightAce and by the messaging service provider as necessary to deliver the conversation.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through chat unless specifically required and handled through an appropriate secure process.

4. Files and Attachments

When you voluntarily submit an assignment or other file for academic assistance, the file may be stored in BrightAce's connected storage systems so authorized personnel can access it for the requested service. We use reasonable measures to limit access to these materials.

5. Payments

BrightAce uses third-party payment services, including Paystack where applicable, to process payments. Payment information may be processed according to the payment provider's privacy policy and terms.

Payment safety: never send payment directly to a tutor's personal bank account, mobile-money account, PayPal account, WhatsApp number, or other personal payment method. Use the official BrightAce payment process for BrightAce services.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, operate our systems, process payments, communicate through connected platforms, protect our services, or comply with legal obligations. Authorized tutors may receive information necessary to perform an assigned academic task. Internal commercial information such as BrightAce's margin or private financial records is not intended to be disclosed to tutors.

7. Data Retention

We may retain messages, request records, payment records, refund records, and submitted materials for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution, security, and legal or regulatory requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may contact BrightAce to ask about information associated with your service interactions or to request correction or deletion where appropriate. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

BrightAce provides academic support for students and may be used by minors with appropriate involvement of a parent, guardian, school, or other responsible adult where required. We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us.

10. Third-Party Services

BrightAce may rely on third-party services for website hosting, messaging, file storage, payment processing, analytics, security, or other operational functions. Those providers may process information according to their own policies and applicable terms.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so please use care when choosing what information to send online.

12. International Users

BrightAce is designed to serve students internationally. Depending on the services used, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to applicable law and relevant provider safeguards.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

14. Privacy Questions and Requests

If you have a privacy question, request, or concern, BrightAce can review the matter through its established support and communication channels. Where appropriate, requests for correction or deletion will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the retention requirements described in this policy.
